Short Description:
This Shiny app, Praha Air Quality Analysis, provides an interactive platform to explore and analyze 
air quality data from Praha 2 - Riegrovy sady for January 2024. Users can select a specific pollutant (NO₂, O₃, PM10, PM2.5) 
and filter the dataset using a week range slider to analyze trends.

The app includes:
🔹 Time Series Trends: Displays smoothed daily air quality variations over January 2024.
🔹 Summary Statistics: Provides key metrics (min, max, mean, median, and standard deviation).
🔹 Histogram & Boxplot: Helps visualize distribution and variability of pollutants.
🔹 Comparison Feature: Enables comparison of all four air quality measures in one plot.
🔹 Location Map: Shows the measurement station in Praha 2 - Riegrovy sady.
🔹 Data Export: Users can download filtered datasets for offline analysis.

Conclusion:
This project helped me explore air quality levels in Praha 2 - Riegrovy sady and how they change over time.
The ability to compare different pollutants made it easier to spot patterns, especially in urban pollution. The high
levels of NO₂ and PM pollutants suggest that traffic and other sources could be major contributors to air pollution.
Using interactive graphs and statistics made it much clearer how air quality fluctuates, and it showed why monitoring
this data is important. Overall, this app is a helpful tool for understanding pollution trends and raising awareness
about environmental issues in the city.
